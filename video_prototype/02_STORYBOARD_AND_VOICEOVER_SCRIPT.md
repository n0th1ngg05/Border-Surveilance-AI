## Technical Director Notes for Post-Production

### 1. Seamless Transition Guide
- **Cut 1 (Drone Lock → Snapshot):** Use a white flash frame (duration: 2 frames) paired with an optical shutter click SFX. In the following frame, freeze the video and shrink it into a 16:9 inner window with a bounding box.
- **Cut 2 (Snapshot → V.I.E.W Console):** Apply a 3D rotation transition (Rotation Y: 25° → 0°) with a quick camera push-in. The bounding box on the snapshot smoothly aligns with the canvas element `#hud-canvas` in the V.I.E.W UI.
- **Cut 3 (UI → Command Center):** Rapid motion blur pan down into the physical monitor of the command center desk, pulling back to reveal the room.
- **Cut 4 (Command Center → Night Intercept):** Hard cut on the radio callout with a sudden burst of high-intensity vehicle headlights flashing across the lens.
- **Cut 5 (Intercept → Keynote Stage):** Cross-dissolve into the dark studio floor reflections with an upward tilt toward the glowing OLED display.

### 2. Audio Stems & Mastering Guide
- **Master LUFS:** -14 LUFS (broadcast standard for web and product presentations).
- **Voiceover Processing:** Neumann U87 or Shure SM7B profile, heavy low-mid proximity boost (120Hz–250Hz), subtle compression (LA-2A style), high-shelf shimmer at 10kHz.
- **SFX Priority:** UI clicks and HUD lock tones should sit at -6dB relative to the voiceover, never overpowering the narration.
