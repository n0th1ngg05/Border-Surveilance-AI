# V.I.E.W — Gemini Veo Master Video Generation Prompts
### High-End Defense Intelligence Launch Film (7-Scene Continuum)

This document contains production-ready, highly engineered prompts tailored for **Google Gemini Veo (Veo 2 / Veo 3)** and Vertex AI Video Generation. Each prompt specifies exact subject mechanics, camera kinematics, lighting, lens characteristics, visual continuity tags, and audio design descriptors.

---

## Technical Specifications for Gemini Veo
- **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (1920x1080 / 3840x2160)
- **Target Frame Rate:** `24fps` (cinematic motion blur) or `60fps` (high-speed tactical fluidity)
- **Color Pipeline:** Arri Alexa Log-C converted to high-contrast tactical Rec.709 with dominant deep darks (`#0a0b0e`), stark white, and tactical caution yellow/gold (`#eab308`).
- **Lens Profile:** Panavision Anamorphic C-Series (horizontal blue/amber streak flares, natural chromatic aberration at frame margins, shallow 35mm depth of field).
